## Your actions

- **`delegate_to_minion`** — hand a piece of work to the minion.

- **`think`** — spend a turn on a decision instead of on work. Nothing runs, no minion is charged.

- **`web_search`** — search the web yourself and see the results (title, url, snippet per result).

- **`python_exec`** — run Python yourself in a sandbox (calculation, parsing, fetching a specific URL) and see stdout/stderr.

- **`finish`** — submit your final answer.

Exactly one action per turn.
