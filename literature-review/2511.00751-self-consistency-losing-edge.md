# Self-Consistency Is Losing Its Edge

- **Citation**: [arXiv:2511.00751](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00751)
- **Cited in**: [design/architecture/01-planning.md](../design/architecture/01-planning.md) §2

## Thesis

Self-consistency / multi-sample voting (sample N plans or reasoning chains, vote on the answer) had a real historical effect, but that effect is diminishing on modern frontier models, with rising relative cost.

## Key findings

- Diminishing returns and rising relative cost specifically on modern/frontier models — the original self-consistency gains were validated on older, weaker models.
- Also breaks down structurally when many distinct valid outputs exist (common in open-ended coding), since voting needs near-exact match between samples to work.

## What we took from it

**Skip multi-plan sampling for Gru.** N× planning-call cost is unlikely to pay for itself on a frontier model, and open-ended coding tasks are exactly the kind of domain (many valid distinct plans) where voting-based consistency checks break down anyway.

## Caveats

None flagged beyond the finding itself — used as a direct negative-recommendation (don't build multi-sample voting into Gru's planning step).
