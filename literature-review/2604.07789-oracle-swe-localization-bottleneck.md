# ORACLE-SWE (Agentless oracle-localization result)

- **Citation**: [arXiv:2604.07789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07789)
- **Cited in**: [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §Investigation: SWE-bench family

## Thesis

Measures how much of agentic SWE performance is bottlenecked on *localization* (finding the right file/lines to change) vs. *code-writing* (writing a correct patch once you know where to change it), by comparing realistic search-based localization against perfect ("oracle") localization.

## Key findings

- Resolve rate on SWE-bench Lite: **28.0% → 40.3% (+12.3pp)** when the system is given perfect localization instead of realistic search.

## What we took from it

**Strong evidence that context-gathering/localization, not code-writing, is the dominant bottleneck** in agentic SWE. Validates this project's core intuition behind splitting menial context-gathering work (which a cheaper minion can do) from high-cognition code synthesis (which needs a stronger model or a well-specified plan) — the biggest lever isn't necessarily "smarter code-writer," it's "better at finding the right place to look."

## Caveats

None flagged — treated as a solid, directly relevant empirical result.
