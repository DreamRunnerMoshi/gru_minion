# PlanBench-XL

- **Citation**: [arXiv:2606.22388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22388)
- **Cited in**: [design/architecture/01-planning.md](../design/architecture/01-planning.md) §1

## Thesis

Benchmarks LLM planning accuracy, including under corrupted or blocked tool access, not just in clean settings.

## Key findings

- Gemini-3.1-Pro tops out at **77.06%** accuracy in clean settings.
- GPT-5.4 gets **51.90%** in block-free settings but **collapses to 11.36%** when relevant tools are blocked or corrupted.

## What we took from it

Planning is fragile to a corrupted or unexpected environment, not just a hard reasoning task in isolation. This informs how much Gru should trust its own upfront plan once minion execution starts hitting unexpected states — a large accuracy drop under tool corruption is a real risk, not a hypothetical one, and is part of the argument for the escalate-on-failure ladder rather than a rigid execute-the-plan-as-written approach.

## Caveats

None specifically flagged in the source design doc beyond the numbers themselves.
