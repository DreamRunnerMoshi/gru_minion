## Thesis

A 2026 successor to [AI Safety via Debate](./1805.00899-ai-safety-via-debate.md) attempting the same PSPACE-style verification guarantee (a weak judge correctly adjudicating claims beyond its own direct-inspection capability), without inheriting debate's obfuscated-argument failure mode.

## Key findings

- Positioned specifically as fixing the obfuscated-arguments problem that undermines the original debate protocol's honesty-favoring equilibrium.

## What we took from it

Flagged as a follow-up worth watching — if this project's debate-escalation step (adopted from the 2018 debate paper) turns out to be vulnerable to the obfuscated-arguments failure mode in practice, this is the candidate successor protocol to evaluate as a replacement or refinement.

## Caveats

Not yet independently evaluated or adopted for this project — noted as a literature pointer for a known weakness in the currently-adopted debate mechanism, not as something already built.
