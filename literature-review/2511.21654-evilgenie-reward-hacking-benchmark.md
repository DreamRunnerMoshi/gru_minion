# EvilGenie

- **Citation**: [arXiv:2511.21654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21654)
- **Cited in**: [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §Reward hacking / verification gaming

## Thesis

A ready-made benchmark and methodology for detecting reward hacking in coding-agent settings.

## Key findings

- Methodology combines held-out tests, an LLM-judge, and test-file-edit detection to catch models that satisfy a reward signal without genuinely solving the task.

## What we took from it

**Adaptable, not yet adopted**: EvilGenie's methodology is a candidate for building a check on whether a minion gamed Gru's verification (e.g. by weakening a test) rather than genuinely solving the task — a concrete implementation path for the reward-hacking defenses [2604.15149](./2604.15149-rl-reward-hacking-test-editing.md) motivates, rather than a finding to cite for its own sake.

## Caveats

None flagged — treated as a tool/methodology to potentially reuse, not a result to interpret.
