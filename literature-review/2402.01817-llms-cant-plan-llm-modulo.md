# LLMs Can't Plan, But Can Help Planning in LLM-Modulo Frameworks

- **Citation**: Kambhampati et al. — [arXiv:2402.01817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01817)
- **Cited in**: [design/architecture/01-planning.md](../design/architecture/01-planning.md) §4

## Thesis

LLMs are unreliable both as autonomous planners *and* as verifiers of their own plans — they frequently accept their own flawed self-generated plans as valid.

## Key findings

- Core recommendation: never trust an LLM's self-assessed plan validity; pair plan generation with an external, ideally deterministic, verifier ("LLM-Modulo").

## What we took from it

**A direct, independent argument for this project's escalate-on-failure / external-verification architecture** — not just for the planning component specifically. Reinforces that Gru's plan shouldn't be trusted just because Gru itself is confident in it; mechanical verification (test runners, FAIL_TO_PASS/PASS_TO_PASS) needs to be the actual gate, consistent with the rest of the project's design.

## Caveats

None flagged — cited as reinforcing evidence for an architecture decision already made on other grounds, not as the sole justification for it.
