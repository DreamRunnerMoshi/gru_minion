Change Summary

- Modified the implementation of the `_arithmetic_mask` method in `nddata_withmixins.py` to correctly handle cases where masks are absent or present, ensuring deep copies are used when both masks exist.
- The change aims to improve mask propagation and prevent potential side effects during arithmetic operations.
- No other parts of the code were altered.

Reason: To ensure consistent and correct handling of masks during NDData arithmetic operations.

Notes: No other issues or deferred work noted.
