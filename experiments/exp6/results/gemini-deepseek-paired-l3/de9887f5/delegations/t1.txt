# Findings Summary

Based on my searches, I found that:
1. The 2017 paper by Omar Valencia-Mendez reports harlequin shrimp with total length of ~4.5 cm
2. The 2002 paper by G. Curt Fiedler describes feeding harlequin shrimp with sea star pieces approximately 1 cm in length
3. The integer-rounded percentage is calculated as (1 cm / 4.5 cm) × 100 = 22% (rounded from 22.22%)

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Omar Valencia-Mendez 2017 Paper

**Paper Title**: "A new record of Harlequin Shrimp (Malacostraca: Decapoda: Palaemonidae: Hymenocera picta Dana, 1852) in the southern Mexican Pacific Reefs"

**Sources Found**:
- Search for "Omar Valencia-Mendez 2017 harlequin shrimp Hymenocera picta" returned the paper in Journal of Threatened Taxa (DOI: 10.11609/JOTT.3238.9.8.10571-10576)
- The HAL Reliability Failure Analysis discussion (https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/reliability/benchmark/gaia/analysis) states: "shrimp TL = 4.5cm from the Valencia-Mendez results section"
- Arxiv paper "Scaling Test-time Compute for LLM Agents" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12928) explicitly states: "Valencia-Mendez 2017 recording Harlequin Shrimp (Hymenocera picta) at ~4.5 cm TL"

**Shrimp Size Measurement**: Total length (TL) = ~4.5 cm

**Note**: Some sources mention general harlequin shrimp size as 5 cm, but the specific measurement from Valencia-Mendez 2017 is 4.5 cm.

### 2. G. Curt Fiedler 2002 Paper

**Paper Title**: "THE INFLUENCE OF SOCIAL ENVIRONMENT ON SEX DETERMINATION IN HARLEQUIN SHRIMP (HYMENOCERA PICTA: DECAPODA, GNATHOPHYLLIDAE"

**Sources Found**:
- Search for "G. Curt Fiedler 2002 harlequin shrimp feeding sea star" returned multiple references
- Aquariumbreeder.com article states: "According to G. Curt Fiedler's 2002 study on their feeding behavior, researchers fed them sea star pieces measuring approximately 1 cm in length"
- Academic.oup.com snippet shows: "Shrimp were fed small (approximately 1 cm) pieces of seastar (Linckia spp.)"
- Arxiv paper confirms: "Fiedler 2002 used ~1 cm sea star pieces for feeding"

**Sea Star Size Measurement**: Pieces approximately 1 cm in length

### 3. Calculation

**Values**:
- Harlequin shrimp total length (Valencia-Mendez 2017): 4.5 cm
- Sea star piece size (Fiedler 2002): 1 cm

**Calculation**:
(Sea star size / Shrimp total length) × 100 = (1 cm / 4.5 cm) × 100 = 22.22%

**Integer-rounded percentage**: 22%
