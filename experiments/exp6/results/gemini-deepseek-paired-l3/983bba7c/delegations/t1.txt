# Findings Report: Papers and Animals in Hafnia alvei Research

## Summary
Analysis of three key papers on Hafnia alvei revealed that only humans are mentioned in both Lagkouvardos's and Tapia's papers. However, humans are not mentioned in the 2021 multicenter randomized double-blind study, which only references rodents. No animal species appears in all three papers.

- **Source**: Wikipedia Spanish page confirms the genus name refers to Hafnia, the ancient Latin name for Copenhagen
- **URL**: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafnia
- **Confirmation**: "Su etimología hace referencia a Hafnia, antiguo nombre latín de Copenhague."

### 7. Conclusion
The only animal mentioned in both Lagkouvardos's and Tapia's papers is humans. However, humans are not mentioned in the 2021 multicenter randomized double-blind study abstract (only rodents are mentioned). Therefore, **no animal species appears in all three papers** based on the accessible content.
