# Findings: Scientists and Predictions in 'The Thinking Machine' (1961)

**Summary:** The 1961 CBS documentary "The Thinking Machine" features interviews with AI pioneers Jerome Wiesner, Oliver Selfridge, and Claude Shannon. Jerome Wiesner predicted thinking machines would arrive in "four or five years," while Claude Shannon predicted "within 10 or 15 years." Wiesner thus made the sooner prediction. Arthur Samuel was also interviewed but no specific timeline prediction from him was found in the searched sources.

## Detailed Findings

### Scientists Interviewed in the Documentary

Based on multiple search results, the following scientists were interviewed in "The Thinking Machine" (1961):

1. **Jerome B. Wiesner** - Director of MIT's Research Laboratory of Electronics, later MIT President
2. **Oliver G. Selfridge** - AI researcher at MIT Lincoln Laboratory, known as the "father of machine perception"
3. **Claude Shannon** - Information theory pioneer, often called the "father of the digital age"

**Additional scientists mentioned in one source:**
- Arthur L. Samuel (IBM, creator of first self-learning checkers program)
- Jerome Y. Lettvin
- Douglas T. Ross  
- Ronald Melzack
- Bärbel Inhelder

### Timeline Predictions for Thinking Machines/AI/Robots

#### Jerome Wiesner's Prediction
- **Prediction:** "four or five years" for machines to actually think
- **Source:** Multiple sources including transcript excerpts and blog analysis
- **Quote context:** "I suspect if you come back in four or five years I'll say sure they really do think"
- **URL Sources:**
  - https://voiceinthemachine.com/2012/06/18/can-machines-really-think
  - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZzqHXFTy68o (YouTube description mentions this quote)
  - Transcript references mentioning "four or five years"

#### Claude Shannon's Prediction  
- **Prediction:** "within 10 or 15 years" for robots resembling science fiction
- **Source:** Analysis articles and transcript references
- **Quote context:** "I confidently expect that within a matter of 10 to 15 year, something will emerge from the laboratories which is not too far from the robot of science fiction fame"
- **URL Sources:**
  - https://www.argmin.net/p/patterns-predictions-and-agi (analysis article)
  - Transcript references containing "10 to 15 year" prediction

#### Oliver Selfridge's Position
- **Prediction:** No specific timeline found, but expressed conviction that "machines can and will think"
- **Source:** Blog analysis and documentary descriptions
- **Quote context:** "has no doubts that 'machines can and will think' even though he is sure that his daughter will never marry a computer"
- **URL Sources:**
  - https://voiceinthemachine.com/2012/06/18/can-machines-really-think
  - YouTube video descriptions

### Which Scientist Predicted Sooner Timeline?

**Jerome Wiesner** made the sooner prediction:
- Wiesner: "four or five years" 
- Shannon: "10 to 15 years"
- Selfridge: No specific timeline found

Wiesner's 4-5 year prediction is significantly sooner than Shannon's 10-15 year prediction.
