# Research Findings: Embroidery from Uzbekistan Painting and The Last Voyage Ocean Liner

## Summary
Research identified that the ocean liner used in "The Last Voyage" film was the SS Ile de France (playing the role of SS Claridon), and found its October 1949 breakfast menu featuring eight fruits. However, research on Janet Fish's "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" (2008) painting yielded only partial information about three fruits (watermelon, pears, lemons) without complete details on all fruits or their clockwise arrangement.

## Detailed Findings

### Research Conducted

**Searches performed:**
1. "Embroidery from Uzbekistan 2008 painting fruits" - Found general information about the painting at Dayton Art Institute
2. "Embroidery from Uzbekistan Janet Fish painting fruits depicted arrangement" - Found YouTube video mentioning watermelon, pears, lemons
3. "Embroidery from Uzbekistan Janet Fish painting watermelon pears lemons fruits bowl" - Confirmed same fruits from YouTube
4. "Embroidery from Uzbekistan Janet Fish painting complete description fruits list" - No complete list found

6. "The Last Voyage SS Claridon ocean liner breakfast menu October 1949" - Confirmed SS Ile de France as Claridon stand-in
7. Multiple searches for complete fruit list and clockwise arrangement in the painting

### Sources Examined

**Primary sources used:**

- YouTube video by Fabienne (Dayton Art Institute museum guide): Confirmed watermelon, pears, lemons in the painting

**Dismissed sources:**
- Pinterest posts: General mentions of Uzbek embroidery art but not specific to Janet Fish painting
- Various art forum pages: General discussions about Janet Fish's work but not specific details about this painting

### Information Found

**SS Ile de France (October 1949 breakfast menu):**
- Confirmed ocean liner used in "The Last Voyage" (playing SS Claridon)
- Complete fruit list: Honey Dew, Grapefruit, Red Raisin, Orange, Pear, White Raisin, Bananas, Apple

**Janet Fish "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" (2008):**
- Confirmed fruits: Watermelon, Pears, Lemons (from YouTube museum guide)
- Location: Dayton Art Institute
- Dimensions: 50 x 70 inches, oil on canvas
- Artist: Janet Fish (American, 1938-2025)
- No complete fruit list found in authoritative sources
- No clockwise arrangement information available

### Information Not Found

1. Complete list of all fruits in "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" painting
2. Clockwise arrangement of fruits starting from 12 o'clock position
3. Detailed description of the painting's composition showing fruit placement
4. Museum catalog entries with comprehensive fruit analysis

### Cross-Reference Analysis

Based on available information, the fruits from Janet Fish's painting that appear on the SS Ile de France October 1949 breakfast menu are:
- **Pears** (appears in both sources)

This is the only fruit that can be definitively cross-referenced between the two sources. Watermelon and lemons from the painting are not listed on the October 1949 breakfast menu.

## Final Answer

Due to incomplete information about the complete fruit list and clockwise arrangement in Janet Fish's "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" painting, the final answer cannot be provided as requested. Only one fruit (pears) can be definitively cross-referenced between the painting and the ocean liner breakfast menu.
