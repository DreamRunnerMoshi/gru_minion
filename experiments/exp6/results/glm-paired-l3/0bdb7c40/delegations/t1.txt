# NASA Astronaut Group 2 Space Flight Time Analysis

## Summary
Charles "Pete" Conrad belonged to NASA Astronaut Group 2, also known as "The New Nine." After analyzing all group members' space flight times, excluding those with zero time in space, Ed White had the minimum time at approximately 97.92 hours (5875 minutes rounded).

## Detailed Analysis

### Step 1: Identify Conrad's Astronaut Group
Charles "Pete" Conrad was selected as part of NASA's second astronaut group in 1962, known as "The New Nine" or "NASA Astronaut Group 2."

### Step 2: Complete Group Membership
The nine members of NASA Astronaut Group 2 were:
- Neil Armstrong
- Frank Borman  
- Charles "Pete" Conrad
- Jim Lovell
- James McDivitt
- Elliot See
- Thomas Stafford
- Ed White
- John Young

### Step 3: Space Flight Times Research
I researched each astronaut's total space flight time:

1. **Neil Armstrong**: 8.58 days (205.92 hours)
   - Source: New Mexico Museum of Space History, NASA
   - Missions: Gemini 8, Apollo 11

2. **Frank Borman**: 19 days, 21 hours, 35 minutes (477.583 hours)
   - Source: collectSPACE obituary, Britannica
   - Missions: Gemini 7, Apollo 8

3. **Charles "Pete" Conrad**: 49 days, 3 hours, 38 minutes (1179.633 hours)
   - Source: National Aviation Hall of Fame, New Mexico Museum of Space History
   - Missions: Gemini 5, Gemini 11, Apollo 12, Skylab 2

4. **Jim Lovell**: 29 days, 19 hours, 5 minutes (715.083 hours)
   - Source: Wikipedia, New Mexico Museum of Space History
   - Missions: Gemini 7, Gemini 12, Apollo 8, Apollo 13

5. **James McDivitt**: 14 days, 2 hours, 56 minutes (338.933 hours)
   - Source: collectSPACE obituary, NASA
   - Missions: Gemini 4, Apollo 9

6. **Elliot See**: 0 hours (never flew to space - died in training accident)
   - Source: NASA Fandom, Wikipedia
   - Status: Died February 28, 1966 before his assigned Gemini 9 mission

7. **Thomas Stafford**: 507 hours, 43 minutes (507.717 hours)
   - Source: Air Force biography, New Mexico Museum of Space History
   - Missions: Gemini 6A, Gemini 9, Apollo 10, Apollo-Soyuz Test Project

8. **Ed White**: 4.08 days (97.92 hours)
   - Source: NASA, Space Center Houston, Astronautix
   - Missions: Gemini 4 (only mission - died in Apollo 1 fire)

9. **John Young**: 835 hours
   - Source: NASA biography, National Air and Space Museum
   - Missions: Gemini 3, Gemini 10, Apollo 10, Apollo 16, STS-1, STS-9

### Step 4: Exclude Zero-Time Astronauts
Elliot See is excluded from analysis as he spent zero time in space due to his death in a training accident before his assigned mission.

### Step 5: Find Minimum Time
After excluding Elliot See, the astronaut with the minimum space flight time is Ed White with 97.92 hours.

### Step 6: Convert to Minutes and Round
Ed White's time: 97.92 hours × 60 = 5875.2 minutes → rounded to 5875 minutes

## Final Result
White;5875
