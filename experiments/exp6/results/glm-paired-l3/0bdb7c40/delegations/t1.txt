NASA Astronaut Group 2 Members and Their Total Time in Space
===========================================================

**Summary:** NASA Astronaut Group 2 consisted of nine members selected in 1962, known as "The New Nine." Among these astronauts, Ed White spent the least time in space with 5,876 minutes, excluding Elliot See who died before his first mission.

**Complete List of NASA Astronaut Group 2 Members:**

1. **Neil Armstrong** - 12,355 minutes (8.58 days)
2. **Frank Borman** - 28,655 minutes (19 days, 21 hours, 35 minutes)
3. **Charles Pete Conrad** - 70,778 minutes (49 days, 3 hours, 38 minutes)
4. **Jim Lovell** - 42,905 minutes (29 days, 19 hours, 5 minutes)
5. **James McDivitt** - 20,336 minutes (14 days, 2 hours, 56 minutes)
6. **Elliot See** - 0 minutes (Died before first space mission - Gemini 9)
7. **Thomas P. Stafford** - 30,463 minutes (507 hours, 43 minutes)
8. **Ed White** - 5,876 minutes (4 days, 1 hour, 56 minutes)
9. **John W. Young** - 50,139 minutes (34 days, 19 hours, 39 minutes)

**Astronaut with Least Time in Space (excluding zero):**
- **Last Name:** White
- **Total Minutes:** 5,876 minutes
