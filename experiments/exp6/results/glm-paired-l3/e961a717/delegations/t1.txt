# Asian Monarchies and Their Coastal Status

## Summary

I found 13 Asian countries with current monarchies, and 12 of them have access to the sea (are coastal countries). Only Bhutan is landlocked among the Asian monarchies.

## List of Asian Countries with Monarchies

From the Wikipedia page "Monarchies in Asia," the following Asian countries currently have monarchies:

1. Kingdom of Bahrain
2. Kingdom of Bhutan  
3. Brunei Darussalam
4. Kingdom of Cambodia
5. Japan
6. Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan
7. State of Kuwait
8. Malaysia
9. Sultanate of Oman
10. State of Qatar
11. Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
12. Kingdom of Thailand
13. United Arab Emirates

## Coastal Status of Asian Monarchies

Which of these countries have access to the sea:

1. **Bahrain** - ✅ Coastal (has 161 km coastline on Persian Gulf)
2. **Bhutan** - ❌ Landlocked (no direct sea access, only agreement with Bangladesh for port access)
3. **Brunei** - ✅ Coastal (has 161 km coastline on South China Sea)
4. **Cambodia** - ✅ Coastal (has 435 km coastline on Gulf of Thailand)
5. **Japan** - ✅ Coastal (island nation with extensive coastline on Pacific Ocean, Sea of Japan, etc.)
6. **Jordan** - ✅ Coastal (has 26 km coastline on Red Sea via Aqaba)
7. **Kuwait** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Persian Gulf)
8. **Malaysia** - ✅ Coastal (has 4,675 km total coastline on multiple seas including South China Sea, Strait of Malacca)
9. **Oman** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Arabian Sea and Gulf of Oman)
10. **Qatar** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Persian Gulf)
11. **Saudi Arabia** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Persian Gulf and Red Sea)
12. **Thailand** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Gulf of Thailand and Andaman Sea)
13. **United Arab Emirates** - ✅ Coastal (has coastline on Persian Gulf)

## Final Count

**12 out of 13 Asian monarchies have access to the sea.**

Only Bhutan is landlocked among the Asian monarchies.
