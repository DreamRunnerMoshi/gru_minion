# U.S. Agricultural Standards Research: July 2, 1959 to August 2023

## Summary

Research found significant changes in U.S. agricultural standards for processed fruits and vegetables from 1959 to 2023, with many dried/dehydrated and frozen/chilled standards being superseded or updated. The 1959 standards document "United States standards for grades of processed fruits, vegetables, and certain other products" serves as the baseline, while modern standards have evolved through multiple revisions and removals.

## Research Findings

### 1959 Standards Found

**Primary Source:**
- "United States standards for grades of processed fruits, vegetables, and certain other products (as of July 2, 1959)" - Internet Archive
- URL: https://archive.org/details/unitedstatesstan14unit_4
- This document contains the baseline standards for dried/dehydrated and frozen fruits and vegetables

**Key Dried/Dehydrated Standards from 1959:**
- Apples, dried (October 24, 1955)
- Apples, dehydrated (low moisture) - July 31, 1977 (superseded 1959 standard)
- Pears, dried (May 24, 1967)
- Various dried fruits and vegetables

**Key Frozen Standards from 1959:**
- Apples, frozen (May 17, 1954)
- Peas, frozen (May 28, 1959)
- Peppers, sweet, frozen (March 13, 1959)
- Peas and Carrots, frozen (March 20, 1955)
- Pineapple, frozen (January 25, 1949)

### Supersession Status by August 2023

**Superseded Standards:**
1. **Dried/Dehydrated Products:**
   - Original dehydrated standards (pre-1977) were superseded by newer standards
   - The 1974 "United States Standards for Grades of Dehydrated (Low Moisture) Peaches" superseded earlier standards
   - Modern commodity specifications (2021-2023) have replaced many 1959 standards

2. **Frozen Products:**
   - Many frozen vegetable standards were updated between 2013-2013 to replace dual nomenclature with single letter grades
   - Standards like frozen asparagus, lima beans, squash were revised
   - Some standards were removed entirely as part of USDA's regulatory cleanup

**Current Status (August 2023):**
- Dried fruit standards: Updated through commodity specifications (2021)
- Frozen vegetable standards: Revised to single-grade system (2013)
- Some standards removed entirely (2019) as part of regulatory efficiency measures
