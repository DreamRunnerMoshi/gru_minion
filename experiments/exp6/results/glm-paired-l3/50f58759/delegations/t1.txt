1. **API Access**: Used Wikipedia API with proper user-agent headers to retrieve revision history
2. **Revision Identification**: Found June 2023 revision IDs for 20 August days (days 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 31)
3. **Content Retrieval**: Retrieved raw wikitext for each revision using the action=raw endpoint
4. **Citation Counting**: Searched for:
   - `{{cite tweet` templates (1 found on August 18)
   - `{{tweet` templates (none found)
   - `twitter.com` URLs (2 found on August 8)
   - `x.com` URLs (none found - expected for June 2023 content)
