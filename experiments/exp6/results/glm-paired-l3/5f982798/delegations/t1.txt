**Key Finding:**
The X-ray burst from SGR J1935+2154 (the galactic magnetar that produced both X-ray bursts and FRBs) was characterised by three peaks with a time span of 30 milliseconds between them. This measurement appears in the context of discussing the INTEGRAL/IBIS-ISGRI time profile of the bright X-ray burst.
