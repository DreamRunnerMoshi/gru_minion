# The measured time span in seconds from the burst-1 diagram in the July 2020 paper

## Summary
The measured time span for burst-1 (B1) from the time profile diagram in arXiv paper 2007.05101 is 866 ±43 microseconds, which equals 0.000866 ±0.000043 seconds. This represents the full-width half-maximum (FWHM) of the burst profile as determined by fitting a Lorentzian distribution to the autocorrelation function of the time series.

## Detailed Findings

### Key Measurements
- **Burst-1 time span**: 866 ±43 microseconds (0.000866 ±0.000043 seconds)
- **Measurement type**: Full-width half-maximum (FWHM) of the burst profile
- **Method**: Lorentzian fit to the autocorrelation function of the time series
- **Diagram reference**: Figure 1 - "Full polarisation profiles and dynamic spectra of the two bursts"
- **Visual representation**: Dark cyan bar in Figure 1, left column (B1)

### Diagram Context
The burst-1 time profile diagram is Figure 1 in the paper, which shows:
- Full polarisation profiles and dynamic spectra of both bursts (B1 and B2)
- B1 is displayed in the left column, B2 in the right column
- Time resolution of 32 microseconds, frequency resolution of 500 kHz
- The dark cyan bar represents the FWHM measurement
- A light cyan bar (2 times the FWHM for B1) is used for fluence integration

### Additional Information
- The bursts are separated in time by approximately 1.4 seconds
- B1 scattering time scale: 315 ±12 microseconds
- B1 fluence: 112 ±22 Jy ms

## Source
arXiv paper 2007.05101 "Detection of two bright radio bursts from magnetar SGR 1935+2154"
