# Ilias Lagkouvardos's Papers on Hafnia alvei

## Summary

Ilias Lagkouvardos has co-authored one academic paper that specifically discusses Hafnia alvei: "An Insight into Goat Cheese: The Tales of Artisanal and Industrial Gidotyri Microbiota" published in Microorganisms in 2023. This paper examines the microbiota of traditional Greek goat cheese and identifies Hafnia alvei as being more prevalent in artisanal-type Gidotyri cheeses with commercial starters. The main animal mentioned in this paper is the goat, as the study focuses on goat cheese production.

## Papers Found

### 1. "An Insight into Goat Cheese: The Tales of Artisanal and Industrial Gidotyri Microbiota"
- **Authors**: Aikaterini Nelli, Brigkita Venardou, Ioannis Skoufos, Chrysoula (Chrysa) Voidarou, **Ilias Lagkouvardos**, Athina Tzora
- **Journal**: Microorganisms, 2023, Volume 11, Issue 1, Article 123
- **DOI**: 10.3390/microorganisms11010123
- **URL**: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-2607/11/1/123

### Key Findings Related to Hafnia alvei:
- Hafnia alvei (OTU 13) tended to be more prevalent in artisanal-type Gidotyri cheeses with commercial starters (Artisanal_CS) compared to other cheese groups (p < 0.10)
- The paper states that "H. alvei is a frequent member of the microbiota in traditional cheeses possibly related to distinct organoleptic properties while also displaying antibacterial activity against foodborne pathogens"
- This is the first study that utilized high throughput sequencing to achieve a more in-depth characterization of the microbiota in Artisanal and Industrial Gidotyri cheeses
