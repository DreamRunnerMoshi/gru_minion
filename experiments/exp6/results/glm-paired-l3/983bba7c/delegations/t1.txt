# Genus Named for Copenhagen with Alvei Species

The genus named for Copenhagen that has alvei species is **Hafnia**, with the species **Hafnia alvei**. Hafnia is named after Hafnia, the Latin name for Copenhagen, Denmark, and H. alvei is the type species of this genus.

## Genus Hafnia

**Etymology**: The genus name "Hafnia" comes from Hafnia, the Latin name for Copenhagen, Denmark. The name was proposed by V. Møller in 1954.

**Taxonomy**: 
- Domain: Bacteria
- Kingdom: Pseudomonadota (formerly Proteobacteria)
- Phylum: Pseudomonadota
- Class: Gammaproteobacteria
- Order: Enterobacterales
- Family: Hafniaceae
- Genus: Hafnia Møller, 1954

**Characteristics**: Hafnia consists of Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic, rod-shaped bacteria. They are commensal organisms found in the human gastrointestinal tract.

## Species Hafnia alvei

**Type Species**: Hafnia alvei Møller, 1954 is the nomenclatural type of the genus Hafnia.

**Etymology**: The species name "alvei" comes from the Latin genitive masculine "alvei", meaning "of a beehive" (reference to the original isolation from bee honey).

**Other Species in Genus**: 
- Hafnia paralvei
- Hafnia psychrotolerans
- "Hafnia protea" 
- "Hafnia proteus" corrig.

**Clinical Significance**: H. alvei is primarily a commensal of the human gastrointestinal tract and is not normally pathogenic. However, it may cause disease in immunocompromised patients. It is often resistant to multiple antibiotics, including the aminopenicillins.

**Historical Context**: Although Møller originally described this genus in 1954, its legitimacy was challenged over the next two decades. The bacterium was often referred to by synonyms such as "Enterobacter alvei", "Enterobacter aerogenes subsp. hafniae", and "Enterobacter hafniae" before being established as Hafnia alvei.

**Phylogenetic Relationships**: Phylogenetic analyses show that H. alvei's nearest neighbor varies depending on the gene analyzed - it may be Serratia plymuthica, Enterobacter cloacae, or K. pneumoniae depending on the molecular marker used.
