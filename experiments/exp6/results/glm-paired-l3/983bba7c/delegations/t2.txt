# Findings: 2021 Article from Hafnia alvei Wikipedia Page

The 2021 article cited on the Hafnia alvei Wikipedia page describes a multicenter, randomized, double-blind study that investigated the probiotic strain H. alvei HA4597® for weight loss in overweight subjects. The study demonstrated that this probiotic significantly improved weight loss outcomes when combined with a moderate hypocaloric diet.

## Article Details

**Title:** The Probiotic Strain H. alvei HA4597® Improves Weight Loss in Overweight Subjects under Moderate Hypocaloric Diet: A Proof-of-Concept, Multicenter Randomized, Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Study

**Authors:** Déchelotte, Pierre; Breton, Jonathan; Trotin-Picolo, Clémentine; Grube, Barbara; Erlenbeck, Constantin; Bothe, Gordana; Fetissov, Sergueï O.; Lambert, Grégory

**Journal:** Nutrients, Volume 13, Issue 6, Article 1902, 2021

**DOI:** 10.3390/nu13061902

## Study Design

This was a 12-week clinical study with the following characteristics:
- **Design:** Prospective, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized
- **Participants:** 236 overweight volunteers (BMI between 25 and 29.9 kg/m²)
- **Intervention:** Oral intake of strain HA4597 versus placebo
- **Dietary intervention:** Both groups followed a moderate hypocaloric diet (-20% caloric restriction)
- **Dosage:** 2 capsules per day of either Hafnia alvei HA4597® or placebo

## Key Findings

1. **Primary efficacy endpoint met:** The primary efficacy endpoint (weight loss) was achieved with a statistically significant difference favoring the Hafnia alvei group.

2. **Significantly improved weight loss outcomes:**
   - 57.7% vs 41.7% of participants lost at least 3% of body weight (p=0.028)
   - 46.2% vs 30.6% of participants lost at least 4% of body weight (p=0.024)

3. **Enhanced satiety:** The probiotic group showed a significant increase in feeling of fullness (p=0.009) compared to both baseline and placebo groups.

## Mechanism of Action

Hafnia alvei HA4597® produces ClpB protein, which acts through molecular mimicry of the satiety hormone alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (alpha-MSH) and helps regulate appetite.

## Conclusion

The probiotic strain H. alvei HA4597® represents an innovative and well-tolerated strategy to enhance the efficacy of dietary advice for the control of excess body weight. This "precision probiotic" approach paves the way for precision medicine and nutrition by identifying responders through a gut microbial-based personalized approach.
