# Findings: Spanish Word Sharing Spelling with Latin Root "caminus"

## Summary

After extensive searching of Spanish dictionaries and linguistic resources, there is no Spanish word that shares the exact same spelling as the Latin root "caminus". The closest Spanish word is "camino" (meaning "path" or "way"), but it is spelled differently and has a different meaning.

## Detailed Findings

### Searched Sources and Results

1. **Latin Dictionaries** (Latin-is-simple, Latin-dictionary.io, Latin-dictionary.net, Online-latin-dictionary.com, Wiktionary)
   - All confirm "caminus" is a Latin word meaning "fireplace," "furnace," "forge," etc.
   - No mention of it being a Spanish word

2. **Spanish Dictionaries and Resources** (Collins Dictionary, BuenoSpanish, Wiktionary)
   - Found "camino" (Spanish word for "path/way") which has etymological connections to Latin "caminus" but different spelling
   - Found translations of Latin "caminus" into Spanish ("estufa," "horno," "chimenea") but these are translations, not Spanish words spelled "caminus"

3. **Spanish Language Authorities** (RAE.es, Wiktionary Spanish entries)
   - No entry found for "caminus" as a Spanish word
   - Only "camino" appears as a legitimate Spanish vocabulary word

4. **Etymological Discussions** (WordReference forums, academic sources)
   - Discussions confirm that "camino" evolved from a different Vulgar Latin word ("camminus") than "caminus"
   - No evidence that "caminus" itself exists as a Spanish word

### Candidates Considered and Dismissed

1. **"camino"** - Dismissed because while it has the same Latin root, it is spelled differently (camino vs caminus) and means "path/way" rather than "fireplace/furnace"

2. **"estufa," "horno," "chimenea"** - Dismissed because these are Spanish translations of the Latin word "caminus," not Spanish words that are spelled "caminus"

3. **"Caminus" as a proper name** - Dismissed because this is a male given name, not a common Spanish vocabulary word

### What Was Not Found

- No Spanish word spelled exactly "caminus" in any Spanish dictionary
- No Spanish vocabulary entry for "caminus" in RAE or other authoritative sources
- No Spanish word that maintains both the spelling and meaning of Latin "caminus"

## Conclusion

The Latin root "caminus" does not exist as a Spanish word with the same spelling. While there are Spanish words with etymological connections ("camino"), and Spanish translations for the Latin word ("estufa," "horno," "chimenea"), none share the exact spelling "caminus".
