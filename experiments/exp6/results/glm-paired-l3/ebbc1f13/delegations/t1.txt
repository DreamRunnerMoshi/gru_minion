The Latin root word of the Yola word "gimlie"

The Latin root word of the Yola word "gimlie" is "caminus". This is derived from Late Latin "caminata", which evolved through Old French "cheminee" and Middle English "chimnay" to become "gimlie" in the Yola dialect, meaning "chimney".

## Detailed Findings

### Etymological Chain
The word "gimlie" in Yola has the following etymological progression:
- **Yola**: gimlie (meaning "chimney")
- **Middle English**: chimnay
- **Old French**: cheminee 
- **Late Latin**: caminata
- **Latin root**: caminus

### Latin Root: caminus
The exact Latin root word is **caminus**, which is a masculine noun of the second declension. According to authoritative sources:

1. **Meaning in Latin**: fireplace, furnace, forge (from Wiktionary)
2. **Etymology**: Borrowed from Ancient Greek κᾶμινος (káminos) 
3. **Other meanings**: poetic "Vulcan's forge", figuratively "fire"
4. **Declension**: Second declension noun (caminus, camini)
