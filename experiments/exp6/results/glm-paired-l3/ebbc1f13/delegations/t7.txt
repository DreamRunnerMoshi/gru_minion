### Dictionary Compilation Practices in the 1990s

**Search terms used:**
- "dictionary citation practices 1990s Collins academic research"
- "bilingual dictionary compilation practices 1990s Collins academic paper"
- "Collins dictionary corpus linguistics 1990s example sentences"

**Results found:**
1. **Collins corpus development** - Collins developed its corpus in the 1980s and was the first dictionary publisher to use a large corpus for lexicographic work (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eDiXoDcFpWU)

2. **BICORD system** - The Bilingual CORpus-enhanced Dictionaries (BICORD) system linked entries in Collins English-French and French-English bilingual dictionaries with corpus data, but this was primarily for English-French rather than Spanish-English (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00981486)

3. **Corpus-based examples** - Collins uses corpus examples automatically selected from multi-billion word collections, but this reflects current practices, not necessarily 1994 methods (https://blog.collinsdictionary.com/how-do-we-create-our-corpus-examples)
