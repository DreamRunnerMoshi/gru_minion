#### Dictionary Methodology and Sources

From the academic sources found, Collins dictionaries employed a corpus-based methodology:

- **Corpus-based approach**: Collins dictionaries use the "Collins Corpus," a vast database of language samples
- **Multi-genre sources**: The corpus includes "fiction, non-fiction, newspapers, websites and social media"
- **Global coverage**: Includes data from "the United States, Australia, South Africa and Canada to show how English is used by native speakers globally"
- **Evolution from earlier methodology**: In the 1980s, Collins collaborated with linguists at the University of Birmingham (led by John Sinclair) to create corpus-based dictionaries
