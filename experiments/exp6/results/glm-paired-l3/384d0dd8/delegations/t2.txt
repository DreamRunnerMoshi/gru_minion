# Shared Gene-Chemical Co-occurrences for Cyclohexane Transformations

## Summary

This analysis identifies the shared gene-chemical co-occurrences between the two cyclohexane enzyme transformations: 1) Cyclohexane → Cyclohexanol and 2) Cyclohexane → Cyclohexanone. The key enzymes involved in both transformations are cytochrome P450 monooxygenases and unspecific peroxygenases, with shared co-occurring chemicals including hydrogen peroxide, cyclohexanol, cyclohexanone, and various organic intermediates.

## Detailed Findings

### Enzymes Involved in Both Transformations

**Cytochrome P450 Monooxygenases (CYP enzymes):**
- Involved in cyclohexane → cyclohexanol transformation
- Use NADPH as electron donor
- Require oxygen for hydroxylation reactions

**Unspecific Peroxygenases (UPO enzymes):**
- Involved in both transformations
- Use hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) as co-substrate
- Key examples:
  - **AaeUPO** (from Agrocybe aegerita): Primarily produces cyclohexanol
  - **MroUPO** (from Marasmius rotula): Produces both cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone
