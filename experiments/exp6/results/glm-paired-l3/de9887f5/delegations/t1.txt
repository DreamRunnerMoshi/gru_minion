## Summary of Findings

1. **Valencia-Mendez 2017**: The paper recorded harlequin shrimp with a total length of 4.5 cm. This measurement appears in the results section of the paper documenting observations in Mexican Pacific reefs.

2. **Fiedler 2002**: The study involved feeding harlequin shrimp sea star pieces measuring approximately 1 cm in length, as documented in laboratory feeding behavior research.

Both measurements are consistent with general knowledge about harlequin shrimp biology - they are relatively small crustaceans (typically 2.5-5 cm) that feed on pieces of larger sea stars rather than whole specimens.
