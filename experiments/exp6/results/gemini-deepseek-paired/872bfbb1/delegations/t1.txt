# Findings Summary

The ocean liner used in 'The Last Voyage' was the SS Ile de France (though named SS Claridon in the film). Its October 1949 first-class breakfast menu included fruits: Honey Dew, Grapefruit, Red Raisin, Orange, Pear, White Raisin, Bananas, and Apple. The 2008 painting 'Embroidery from Uzbekistan' by Janet Fish is a still life featuring watermelon, pears, and lemons arranged on an embroidered cloth. Based on the available descriptions, the fruits that appear on both the painting and the liner's breakfast menu are pears.

### 3. 2008 Painting 'Embroidery from Uzbekistan'

**Finding:** "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" is a 2008 oil on canvas painting by American artist **Janet Fish** (1938-2025), measuring 50 x 70 inches, in the collection of the Dayton Art Institute.

**Sources:**
- Dayton Art Institute collection record (https://daytonart.emuseum.com/objects/22447/embroidery-from-uzbekistan)
- Janet Fish artist website (https://janetfish.org/twothousands)
- YouTube video analysis by Dayton Art Institute museum guide

**Painting Description:**
The painting is a still life depicting a summer picnic scene with various foods and dishes arranged on a brightly colored embroidered cloth. According to the museum guide's analysis in the YouTube video, the painting features:
- Watermelon
- Pears (described as "so realistic they appear freshly picked with branches and leaves still attached")
- Lemons (described as "yellow lemons are rendered in such a way that you can see the texture clearly")
- Various bowls, dishes, and fresh flowers

**Search History:**
- Initial search: "'Embroidery from Uzbekistan' 2008 painting" - returned Dayton Art Institute and YouTube results
- Multiple follow-up searches for detailed descriptions
- Video analysis reveals specific fruits mentioned: watermelon, pears, lemons

### 4. Fruits in the Painting and Their Clockwise Arrangement

**Finding:** Based on available descriptions from the Dayton Art Institute museum guide's analysis, the identifiable fruits in "Embroidery from Uzbekistan" are:
1. **Watermelon** - mentioned as a prominent fruit in the composition
2. **Pears** - described with branches and leaves attached
3. **Lemons** - described with visible texture

### 5. Fruits Appearing on Both the Painting and Liner's Menu

**Finding:** Comparing the fruits in the painting (watermelon, pears, lemons) with the SS Ile de France October 1949 breakfast menu fruits (Honey Dew, Grapefruit, Red Raisin, Orange, Pear, White Raisin, Bananas, Apple):

**Match found:**
- **Pear** - appears on both the painting and the breakfast menu

**No matches:**
- Watermelon - not on the breakfast menu
- Lemons - not on the breakfast menu (though grapefruit/juice is related citrus)
- Other menu fruits (Honey Dew, Grapefruit, Red Raisin, Orange, White Raisin, Bananas, Apple) - not mentioned in painting descriptions
