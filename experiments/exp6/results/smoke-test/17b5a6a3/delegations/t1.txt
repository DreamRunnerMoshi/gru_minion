# USGS Data on Nonnative Clownfish (Amphiprion ocellaris) Occurrences Before 2020

Based on my search of the USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species (NAS) database, I found that the clownfish Amphiprion ocellaris had one documented nonnative occurrence in the United States before 2020.

## Summary

According to USGS NAS database records, Amphiprion ocellaris was documented as nonnative in Florida in 2018. The occurrence was recorded in the Crystal-Pithlachascotee hydrologic unit area, which corresponds to Crystal River, Florida. This location has three associated five-digit zip codes.

## Findings

The following five-digit zip codes represent locations where clownfish (Amphiprion ocellaris) was documented as nonnative according to USGS data before 2020:

- **34423** - Crystal River, Florida
- **34428** - Crystal River, Florida  
- **34429** - Crystal River, Florida
