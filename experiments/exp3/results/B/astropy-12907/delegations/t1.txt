# Separability-semantics dependencies in the astropy codebase

Scope: read-only exploration under `/testbed/astropy` (which is the root of the
`astropy` package; e.g. `modeling/separable.py` lives at
`/testbed/astropy/modeling/separable.py`).

Two unrelated "separable" concepts exist in the tree. Only the **modeling**
one (`astropy.modeling.separable`) is relevant to the `_cstack` fix in
`separable.py`. The `_separable` / `.separable` hits in `convolution/` and
`is_separable` in `visualization/wcsaxes/` are different domains (see
"Dismissed candidates" below).

The core machinery lives in `modeling/separable.py`:
- `_cstack(left, right)` — the `'&'` operator handler — `separable.py:219-247`
- `_coord_matrix(...)` — `separable.py:171-216`
- `_cdot`, `_arith_oper`, `_separable`, `_operators` — `separable.py:250-317`
- public API `is_separable` (`:27-63`) and `separability_matrix` (`:66-102`),
  both of which funnel into `_separable` → `_operators[op]` → `_cstack`.
- `_separable` (`:290-311`) dispatches nested `CompoundModel`s via
  `_operators[transform.op]`, so a nested `'&'` operand is exactly where
  `_cstack` handles an ndarray operand (the `else` branches at `:238-245`).
- `Model._calculate_separability_matrix` hook — `core.py:808-815`; the
  `Model.separable` property reads `self._separable` — `core.py:1534-1543`;
  `Model._separable` default (`None`) — `core.py:655`.

## (3) Tests constructing a compound model with a NESTED compound operand
## using '&' and checking separability

- `modeling/tests/test_separable.py` — `test_separable_nested_compound`
  (`:153-163`) is the **only** such test:
  ```
  153  def test_separable_nested_compound():
  154      # Regression test: a compound model nested as an operand of '&' must
  155      # give the same separability matrix as the equivalent flat compound
  156      # model, whether the nested model is the left or right operand.
  157      nested = separability_matrix(rot & (sh1 & sh2))
  158      flat = separability_matrix(rot & sh1 & sh2)
  159      assert_allclose(nested, flat)
  160
  161      nested = separability_matrix((sh1 & sh2) & rot)
  162      flat = separability_matrix(sh1 & sh2 & rot)
  163      assert_allclose(nested, flat)
  ```
  This is the direct regression guard for the nested-operand branches of
  `_cstack` (`separable.py:238-245`).
- `modeling/tests/test_separable.py` — `test_separable`'s `compound_models`
  include multi-`&` chains (e.g. `cm1` `sh1 & sh2 & sh1` at `:32`), but Python
  chains these into a **flat** left-associated compound, not a nested
  `(&)`-as-operand, so they are not nested-operand tests. No other test in the
  tree constructs `X & (A & B)` or `(A & B) & X` and asserts separability.
